What the reveal actually demonstrates Every figure on this page is computed from data/processed/crashes.parquet, not typed. Belt Pkwy falls from rank 1 to effectively off the list. Every highway does. The surface streets stay put.
  • The standard view shows eight NYC highways with ZERO traffic deaths. Belt Pkwy 0 (actually 56) · Grand Central 0 (23) · FDR Drive 0 (22) · Cross Bronx 0 (20) · BQE 0 (19) · Long Island Expy 0 (27) · Van Wyck 0 (12) · Major Deegan 1 (30). That is 209 deaths rendered as 1.
  • Across all 12 featured corridors the standard view hides 225 of 288 deaths.
  • Highways lose 88–98% of their crashes: Belt Pkwy 97.6%, Long Island Expy 97.2%, BQE 97.1%, FDR Dr 95.5%, Major Deegan 94.0%, Grand Central Pkwy 91.6%, Van Wyck 90.8%, Cross Bronx 88.1%. Surface streets barely move: Broadway 28.8%, Atlantic Ave 26.2%, Flatbush Ave 22.7%, Linden Blvd 22.4%.
  • So the standard view does not merely undercount — it systematically erases the highway network, which is exactly where the fatality rate is highest (3.191 vs 1.872 per 1,000, a 1.70× difference).
  • Design note: the completeness channel stays neutral + hatch per DESIGN.md §1. It never borrows a severity hue, so “dropped” can never read as “dangerous”.
